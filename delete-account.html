Delete your GenZ Code account

You can delete your account without reinstalling the app. This permanently deletes:

  • your synced learning data (saved words, scores, review progress, profile name and avatar choice),
  • your pending word suggestions, reports and feedback,
  • your support directory entry and your sign-in account.

Kept: security events without personal details (auto-deleted after 90 days) and, until deletion is confirmed, a marker with only your account ID. Learning data stored only on a phone is removed in the app (You → Threat Shield → Delete all data on this device) or by uninstalling. See the privacy policy.

Prefer to do it in the app? Open You → My account → Delete account and cloud data. Can’t sign in? E-mail PUBLISHER: support e-mail from your account address with the subject “Delete my account”.
