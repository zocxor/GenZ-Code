The short version

  • Messages you decode are processed only on your device. They are never saved, logged or sent anywhere, and are cleared when the app goes to the background.
  • You can use GenZ Code without an account. Your learning data then stays on your device.
  • If you create an account, your learning data is backed up to your account so it survives reinstalling and syncs between your devices.
  • We do not use advertising, advertising IDs, third-party analytics or AI providers, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Deleting your data

  • On your device: You → Threat Shield → Delete all data on this device.
  • Your account and its cloud data: You → My account → Delete account and cloud data, or on the web at delete-account.html.

Security

All connections use HTTPS/TLS. Your sign-in session is stored in the device’s encrypted secure storage. Access to account data is restricted by server-side security rules: only you can read your learning data; a small number of authorised staff can view account records for support, using two-step verification, and every staff action is logged. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users of a breach as required by law.
